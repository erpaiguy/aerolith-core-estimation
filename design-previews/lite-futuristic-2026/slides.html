Problem

The product still reads as mixed chrome

Navy rail, paper grey, orange and blue competing. Estimators need one operable surface: light, high contrast, keyboard-first, tables first.

Shared contract

Same rooms. Same process. New skin.

Light only

White / lite paper. No dark rail. Accent used for selection, focus, and one primary action.

Operable

Visible focus ring. 32–36px hits. Tab order matches the visual order. Reduce-motion safe.

Process intact

Summary owns Pack, Send, Receive, Award. Settle stays a checklist from Summary.

Three directions

How they differ

Density of chrome vs air around the book. Higher is tighter estimating. Lower is more consumer-SaaS.

Option A

Ice Paper

Swiss top tabs, ice cyan, Inter, 4px corners. Closest to today’s density. Best if the book must stay dense.

Option B

Cloud Speed

Warm white, Plus Jakarta, 12px corners, 64px icon rail. Best if the goal is “feels fast and friendly” on long days.

Option C

White Horizon

Mint paper, teal, orange only on Settle, process stepper always visible. Best if sequence and sealing should never get lost.
