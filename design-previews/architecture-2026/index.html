Document architecture: a job is a database you edit, not an object you hold

Every decision below follows from two of your answers: 600,000 rows must stay usable, and a job file must behave like a document you open, edit, undo, save and reopen. Those two together mean the whole job can no longer live in the browser's memory.

Diagram 1 — Runtime architecturewhere the data actually lives

One window per job. The job lives in SQLite, not in React state.

Today the entire project sits in a single React state object that is cloned on every keystroke. That is the root cause of the scale ceiling, and it is also why undo has nowhere to attach.

New in this architecture Rewritten from what exists Roughly as today
Diagram 2 — What happens when someone types a numberthe path every edit takes

Every edit is a command. That single rule gives undo, autosave, the change log and incremental recompute at once.

Nothing is allowed to modify the job except through this path — which is enforced by the data layer exposing no other way in.

Diagram 3 — Structure and the three BOQ shapes you describedsheets and sub-categories become real levels

One hierarchy, three ways of totalling it

Today a bill holds a flat list of items and “category” is just a text field, so sheet and sub-category totals are impossible. Making them real levels is what unlocks all three of the shapes you described — and the format stays the user's to configure.

The hierarchyevery level can carry a total
  • L0Projectone .aero file, one tender
  • L1BOQone per imported worksheet, or created by hand
  • L2Sheetnew — a division within a BOQ, often one per trade
  • L3Categorywas a text field, becomes a real node
  • L4Sub-categorynew — needed for the deepest tender shape
  • L5Itemthe priced line
A page is not a level. Pages come from pagination at print time, which is why a page total can appear inside any of the shapes without changing the data.

Diagram 4 — Revisions and scenarioshistory is sequential, scenarios are parallel

Base and every scenario live in the file, at every revision

A scenario stores only what it changes. Promoting one does not destroy the other — the outgoing base becomes a scenario, so nothing you priced is ever thrown away by a decision.

Nine stages, each one shippable and reversible

The foundation stages are unglamorous but everything else depends on them. Until an old module is migrated, it keeps working through a compatibility layer that hands it the shape it expects.
